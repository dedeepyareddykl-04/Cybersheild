🚨 Cybercrime Victim Assistance & Reporting Dashboard

If you have lost money to UPI fraud, phishing, fake apps, or online extortion, report within the first 2 hours to freeze the scammer's bank account.

📞

Option 1: Call National Cyber Helpline

1930

Available 24x7 across India (operated by I4C / Ministry of Home Affairs). Calling 1930 immediately triggers a financial freeze request on the fraudster's account.

📞 Dial 1930 Now

Option 2: Report on Official Govt. Portal

cybercrime.gov.in

File an official online complaint with the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal for financial fraud, social media hacking, or cyberbullying.

🌐 Open Official Govt. Website ↗

📋 Keep These Ready Before Calling 1930 or Filing Online

1. Transaction Details 12-digit UTR / Reference Number, exact amount lost, date, and time of transaction.
2. Account Information Your bank name/account number and the scammer's UPI ID, phone number, or website link.
3. Screenshots & Proof Screenshots of the payment receipt, WhatsApp/SMS chat, and call logs (do not delete anything).
4. Freeze Your Card/UPI Immediately call your bank's customer care to block compromised cards or net banking.
